# Crownline v1.1 Candidate — Playtest Log

This log records human-play evidence for the frozen **Experimental · Crownline v1.1 Candidate**.

The purpose is not to accumulate feature requests. The candidate is frozen. New entries should record what actually happened in play, especially anything that may justify reopening the rules or, conversely, support promotion to Official v1.1.

## Recording rule

For each complete two-game set, record only what was actually observed.

Useful evidence includes:

- set score and winner;
- approximate game length or pacing impression;
- promotions and whether Kings created meaningful decisions;
- Crownlines scored, including Royal Crownlines;
- whether cooldown or line retirement materially affected strategy;
- Sovereign capture refusals that changed the position;
- repetition/stalling behavior;
- any move that felt forced by the rules but strategically nonsensical;
- any exploit or scoring loop;
- any moment where the interface/rules explanation was unclear.

Do not change the rules because of a single surprising position. Record it first and look for recurrence.

## Promotion signal

Promotion becomes appropriate when several additional complete sets produce varied positions and no recurring structural problem appears.

A candidate set is especially useful evidence when it contains one or more of the following:

- a meaningful Sovereign choice;
- at least one Crownline attempt or denial;
- cooldown influencing route planning;
- retired lines changing which geometry a player pursues;
- a late-game King decision under capture pressure;
- a close aggregate result across the two-game set.

## Promotion blockers

Any repeatable example of the following should stop promotion pending investigation:

- Crownline farming or another scoring exploit;
- trivial avoidance of tactical consequences through Sovereignty;
- severe or recurring repetition;
- a rule forcing obviously inferior play without a meaningful tradeoff;
- Crownline scoring becoming either irrelevant or overwhelmingly dominant;
- Royal +30 creating disproportionate outcomes;
- persistent color/seat bias across complete sets;
- a scoring result that remains unintuitive even after accurate onboarding.

## Freeze milestone — 2026-08-16

**Status:** candidate rules frozen.

Human play leading into the freeze exposed and resolved the following issues:

- mandatory King capture conflicted with deliberate Crownline construction;
- permanent meld-piece exhaustion made later genuine Crownlines feel unrewarded;
- reusable pieces without line retirement allowed repeated Crownline farming;
- King-gated Crownlines strengthened the thematic and strategic role of promotion;
- three-turn piece cooldown plus per-player line retirement produced a cleaner scoring cycle;
- Sovereign King movement and Crowned Meld scoring complemented one another in live play.

The combined profile was then played and no immediate structural problem was identified. Development therefore moved from rule invention to observation.

### Current frozen candidate

- ordinary pieces remain capture-bound;
- Kings are Sovereign and may decline mandatory capture;
- a Crownline requires at least one King;
- normal Crownline = +15;
- three Kings = Royal Crownline +30;
- scoring pieces receive a 3-turn Crownline cooldown;
- each Crownline geometry scores once per player per game;
- scored geometry retires only for the player who scored it;
- recovered pieces may later score a different unretired line;
- Official v1.0 set structure, capture quota, final response, complementary Game 2, and aggregate scoring remain otherwise intact.
